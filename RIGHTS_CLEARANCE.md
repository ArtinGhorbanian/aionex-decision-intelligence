# Rights and Competition Clearance

- Status: **public and commercial release blocked**
- Reviewed: 2026-08-08
- Repository mode allowed by this review: **private pre-release only**

This is an engineering due-diligence record, not legal advice.

## Evidence reviewed

The workspace was inspected for version-control history, license files,
notices, contributor records, authorship declarations, competition or
hackathon references, organizer rules, embargoes, team records, asset
provenance, and provider terms.

The review found:

- no existing Git history or earlier commits;
- no prior `LICENSE`, `NOTICE`, contributor agreement, assignment, or authorship
  record;
- no reliable evidence identifying the original author or every contributor;
- no substantive competition, hackathon, organizer, prize, team, or embargo
  record;
- no provenance record for the existing inline SVG symbols or favicon;
- runtime integrations whose commercial and attribution conditions differ by
  provider and, for some providers, by plan.

No competition origin was detected in the available files. Absence of evidence
is not proof that the work was never entered in a competition or created with a
team.

## Ownership conclusion

The available evidence does not establish that AIONEX is the sole copyright
owner or has every right needed for public or commercial distribution. AIONEX
may be used as the product and maintainer brand, but the repository must not say
“Copyright AIONEX,” “All Rights Reserved by AIONEX,” or otherwise imply sole
ownership until that conclusion is supported in writing.

The temporary [LICENSE](LICENSE) therefore grants no license and preserves the
position of all verified rightsholders. It is intentionally not the proposed
“AIONEX Proprietary Source License”; that license would be inaccurate before
clearance.

## Conditions for public release

Before changing repository visibility or distributing the product:

1. Identify the original author and every contributor, including work produced
   before the AIONEX presentation.
2. Collect written assignments, licenses, contributor agreements, or other
   grants covering code, copy, designs, datasets, and assets.
3. Determine whether the project originated in a competition, classroom,
   employment, client, or team setting; obtain and archive the applicable rules
   and permissions.
4. Confirm provenance and permitted use of all SVG artwork and replace anything
   that cannot be documented.
5. Complete provider-by-provider commercial-use review, including the active
   CoinGecko plan and linked attribution, Stack Exchange attribution, World Bank
   indicator-specific rights, EU source acknowledgement, and API rate/usage
   terms.
6. Decide which party can license the verified original portions and which
   notices or contributor attributions must be preserved.
7. Replace the temporary no-license notice with a final license reviewed against
   the collected evidence.
8. Record the approval, reviewer, date, evidence locations, and any continuing
   restrictions in this file without exposing private personal data.

## Branding and organizer restrictions

No AIONEX organization, trademark registration, incorporation, or organizer
endorsement has been verified. Do not use third-party logos in AIONEX branding,
and do not describe public-sector or community data providers as partners.

## Gate decision

Public release is **not legally cleared**. Private technical work, testing, and
an access-controlled GitHub repository are permitted by this review because
they do not represent public licensing or distribution. Any later visibility
change requires the checklist above to be completed first.
